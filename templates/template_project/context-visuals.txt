# Visual Assets

Use only the declared names below in generated Ren'Py scripts.

## Sprites
- ruby school: Ruby's neutral classroom expression.
- ruby school happy: Ruby is warm, encouraging, or pleased.
- ruby school sad: Ruby is disappointed, worried, or discussing a setback.
- ruby school flustered: Ruby is embarrassed, surprised, or emotionally overwhelmed.

## Backgrounds
- bg classroom: The primary teaching setting.
- bg campus: Outdoor school or transition scenes.

The player character MC has no sprite. Keep sprite placement readable and hide or replace sprites when the speaker's expression changes.
